Privacy Policy

At American Fly, we are committed to protecting your privacy and the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you use our website or services. By accessing americanfly.co.uk or engaging with our team, you agree to the terms of this policy.

01 What Personal Information We Collect

We may collect personal information including, but not limited to:

  • Full name
  • Contact details (email, phone number)
  • Date of birth
  • Passport and identification details
  • Payment and billing information
  • Travel preferences and booking history

This information is essential to provide you with a seamless travel experience.

02 Why We Collect Your Information

1. To Process Bookings and Service Requests

Your personal details are required to book flights, issue tickets, and fulfil travel-related services. Without this data, we cannot complete your travel arrangements.

2. For Timely Communication

We use your contact details to provide booking confirmations, flight updates, changes, cancellations, and customer support.

3. To Offer Personalized Experiences

We analyse your preferences to recommend tailored travel packages, promotional offers, and services that match your interests.

4. Legal and Compliance Obligations

We collect, and store data as required by law and to enforce our terms and conditions.

03 Sharing of Personal Information

We may share your data with trusted third parties such as:

  • Airlines and travel service providers
  • Payment processors
  • Tour operators and transfer companies

All third parties are bound by confidentiality agreements and are only authorized to use your information to fulfil your bookings or services.

04 Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, while we take every reasonable precaution, we cannot guarantee absolute security for information shared online.

05 Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose it was collected for, or as required by legal or regulatory obligations.

06 Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and tracking tools to enhance user experience, analyse site traffic, and deliver personalized content. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. Please note, disabling cookies may impact site functionality.

07 Third-Party Links

Our site may include links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external sites. We recommend reviewing their individual privacy policies.

08 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time without prior notice. The latest version will always be available on our website. We encourage users to review this policy regularly.

09 Contact Us

For any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or how your data is used, please contact us at:

📧 info@americanfly.co.uk
