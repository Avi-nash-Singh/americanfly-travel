Looking for the best things to do in Dubai?

Whether you're visiting for a short vacation or planning a long stay, Dubai offers an exciting mix of adventure, luxury, culture, and relaxation. Here are the top attractions and experiences to include in your itinerary:

Burj Khalifa – World's Tallest Building

Visit the iconic Burj Khalifa and enjoy panoramic views from the 124th or 148th floor. A must-do in Dubai!

Dubai Mall – Shopping & Entertainment

Explore one of the world's largest malls, home to 1,200+ stores, the Dubai Aquarium, and endless dining options.

Desert Safari – Dune Bashing & BBQ Dinner

Go on a thrilling desert safari that includes 4x4 dune bashing, camel rides, sandboarding, live shows, and a traditional Emirati dinner.

Dhow Cruise – Dubai Marina or Creek

Experience a romantic evening cruise on a traditional dhow boat with buffet dinner and city views.

Dubai Creek & Old Souks

Take a traditional abra boat ride and visit the Gold Souk and Spice Souk to explore Dubai's heritage.

JBR, Kite Beach & La Mer

Enjoy sun, sea, and water sports at some of Dubai's most famous public beaches.

Ski Dubai – Indoor Snow Park

Ski or snowboard in the desert at this unique indoor snow resort located in the Mall of the Emirates.

Theme Parks – Family Fun

Visit IMG Worlds of Adventure, Dubai Parks & Resorts, Motiongate, and LEGOLAND for unforgettable family fun.

Note: Excursions and airport transfers are optional and can be added upon request.

Things to do in Dubai

  • Explore the vibrant neighborhoods of Old Dubai, including Al Fahidi Historical District and Al Seef, where you can wander through traditional wind-tower houses, shop in bustling souks, and enjoy Emirati cuisine along the creek.
  • Visit the iconic Burj Khalifa, the world's tallest building. Head up to the observation deck for breathtaking views of the city skyline, or dine at At.mosphere for a sky-high culinary experience.
  • Go on a desert safari and enjoy dune bashing, camel rides, sandboarding, and traditional Bedouin-style entertainment under the stars. Choose between morning, evening, or overnight experiences.
  • Spend a day at Dubai Marina and JBR, where you can stroll the waterfront promenade, enjoy beach activities, or dine at world-class restaurants with stunning views of the sea and skyscrapers.
  • Discover the grandeur of the Dubai Mall, one of the largest shopping and entertainment complexes in the world. Don't miss the Dubai Aquarium, indoor ice rink, and the mesmerizing Dubai Fountain show outside.
  • Take a boat ride around the Palm Jumeirah or visit Atlantis The Palm for its famous waterpark, aquarium, and luxury dining experiences. For a thrill, try skydiving over the palm for unmatched aerial views.

Dubai offers a wide range of experiences year-round, but the weather plays a major role in planning your trip. Here's a guide to help you choose the best time to visit:

The most pleasant weather is from November to March, when temperatures range between 20°C to 30°C—ideal for sightseeing, beach outings, and outdoor activities. However, this is also peak tourist season, so prices may be higher.

April and October offer warm but tolerable weather with fewer crowds, making them good shoulder-season months for a more relaxed visit.

If you're looking for budget travel and don't mind the heat, May to September brings scorching temperatures (often above 40°C) but great deals on hotels and attractions.

Ramadan, a holy month, changes each year but is worth experiencing if you want cultural insight. Just be mindful of public restrictions on eating and drinking during daylight hours.

Regardless of when you go, Dubai is a dazzling destination. Just plan accordingly, especially when it comes to dressing modestly and staying hydrated during warmer months.

Dubai is considered one of the safest cities in the world for tourists. With low crime rates, strict laws, and a strong security presence, visitors can enjoy a peaceful and secure environment.

Nevertheless, it's important to respect local laws and customs. Avoid public displays of affection, dress modestly in certain areas, and refrain from using offensive language or gestures.

Tourists should also be cautious of taxi scams or unlicensed tour operators. Always use registered services and agree on prices beforehand if not using a meter.

Dubai has excellent road safety, but always wear seatbelts and avoid jaywalking. Public transport is clean and efficient, and apps like Careem and Uber are widely used.

The city is well-equipped to handle emergencies, with top-tier hospitals and multilingual staff. Still, travel insurance is recommended to cover any unexpected situations.

Overall, Dubai is a very safe and welcoming destination, especially for solo travelers and families, as long as visitors remain respectful and informed.

The flight duration from the UK to Dubai varies depending on the departure airport and whether you opt for a direct or connecting flight. On average, a direct flight from London Heathrow to Dubai International Airport (DXB) takes around 6 hours and 45 minutes.

Connecting flights can take anywhere from 9 to 15 hours depending on layovers. Major UK cities such as Manchester, Birmingham, and Glasgow also offer direct flights to Dubai.

Since Dubai is a major global hub, there are multiple flights daily from various UK airports, making it a highly accessible destination. Always compare timings and prices to find the most convenient option.

Several major airlines operate direct and connecting flights from the UK to Dubai. Emirates offers direct flights from London Heathrow, London Gatwick, Manchester, Birmingham, and Glasgow to Dubai International Airport.

Other airlines such as British Airways, Qatar Airways, Etihad Airways, Turkish Airlines, and Lufthansa also provide services with layovers in their respective hubs.

With frequent flight options, competitive pricing, and multiple classes (economy, business, and first), travelers have plenty of flexibility. It's advisable to book in advance, especially during peak seasons like December, New Year, or the Dubai Shopping Festival.
