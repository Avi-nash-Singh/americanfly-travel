Who We Are

American Fly operates under the trading name FLY2WORLD UK LTD, headquartered at 450 Bath Road, West Drayton, England, UB7 0EB. We are a UK-based travel agency offering expert booking services for destinations across the globe. While we cater to all major international routes, we take pride in offering exceptional deals—thanks to our trusted supplier network—particularly for travel to the United States, South America, and the Caribbean.

In addition to flights, we also provide a wide range of travel services, including:

  • Tailor-made holiday packages
  • Cruises
  • Airport transfers
  • Car parking
  • Private tours

Our experienced team combines deep industry knowledge with strong partnerships to secure the best possible value for our clients. Whether you're traveling for business or leisure, on a budget or in pursuit of luxury, our mission is to make your journey seamless, comfortable, and truly memorable.
